## What is Pandaqi?

Pandaqi is a one-person studio that makes **free print-and-play board games** and **local multiplayer video games**. Everything is built for the same kind of evening: a few people around a table (or one laptop), a rulebook you can explain in five minutes, and a rematch nobody planned for.

No account. No launcher. No online multiplayer you have to schedule a week in advance. Print the game and play it tonight, or open it in a browser and hand everyone a phone.

## How to start

1. **Pick something that looks fun.** The [search page](/search/) filters every game by player count, playtime, difficulty, genre or platform.
2. **Print it, or open it.** Board games come with printable files (and often a generator for a custom version). Video games run straight in your browser.
3. **Play tonight.** Every game has a full rulebook online, most take half an hour or less, and all of them are free to print, play and share.

## Questions I get a lot

### Are the games really free?

Yes. Every game on this site is free to download, print and play, and every rulebook is free to read. No accounts, no paywalls, no "premium edition" that hides the fun parts.

### Do I need a printer?

For most board games, yes — but plenty of them fit on a single sheet of paper, need only a pen and some scraps, or work with a standard deck of cards. The "What's included" list on a game's page tells you exactly what you need before you commit.

### Can I play alone?

Some games are 1-player by design, and a few were built specifically for it. Filter by player count on the [search page](/search/) to see what works solo.

### Where do I start if I have no idea?

Try one of the spotlights at the top of this page: one is a fresh pick each week, the other is whatever everyone else is playing right now. Both are good excuses to print something tonight.
