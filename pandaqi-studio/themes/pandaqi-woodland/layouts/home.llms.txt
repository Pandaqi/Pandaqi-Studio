# {{ site.Title }}

> Pandaqi is an independent game studio that makes free print-and-play board games and local multiplayer video games. Every game has its own page with a description, screenshots, player count, playtime, a full rulebook and a free download or browser link. No accounts and no paywalls.

## Start here

- [Board games]({{ "/categories/board-game/" | absURL }}): every print-and-play board and card game
- [Video games]({{ "/categories/video-game/" | absURL }}): every browser and local multiplayer game
- [Search and filter]({{ "/search/" | absURL }}): filter the whole catalogue by player count, playtime, difficulty and genre
- [Game collections]({{ "/categories/" | absURL }}): series such as Naivigation, Keebble and The Domino Diaries
- [Tools for game night]({{ "/game-buddy/" | absURL }}): timer, dictionary, dice and genre lists
