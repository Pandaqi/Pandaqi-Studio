Songwriting

This guide is about writing music. It’s a general guide. You can write pop songs with it, but also perhaps film scores or songs for musical theatre.

I’m a professional musician under the name Tiamo el Troubadour.

Frankly, music mixing is my weak spot. You’ll hear that, if you have any critical ear for music.

But songwriting? I am confident I can give you many, many golden nuggets of wisdom :)

Prerequisites: Pen and paper. Some ability to sing or play an instrument.
Category: this course is part of Audio.

What do I need?

Technically, all you need for songwriting is something to write down the song. Pen on paper works. Typing on a laptop as well. In fact, writing isn’t even necessary! You could record yourself playing / singing your song ideas.
Songwriting Audio

Where do I start?

The short answer is: anywhere you like. Especially with something as broad and emotional as music, any starting point is fine. You might be inspired by a book, or a movie, or what someone said to you in that conversation, or by the singing of the birds.
