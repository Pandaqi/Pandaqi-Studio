Verbs: continuous tense

The continuous tense is named quite well. It describes an action that is continuing to happen. The simple tense described an action in a general way. The continuous tense clearly states an action that is going on at a clear point in time.
English (Grammar) Writing

Verbs: perfect tense

We have seen two tenses so far. Those are the building blocks of English: Simple tense: for general truths or predictions about present, past and future Continuous tense: for actual specific actions happening at a clear point in time (present, past or future) What’s missing? Something that happened but is now done. Continuous might talk about something that started and is still going on. Perfect talks about something that used to be going on, but is now over.
