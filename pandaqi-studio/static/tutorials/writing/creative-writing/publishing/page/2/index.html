Publishing

This course explains how to go from finishing a work to actually publishing it. It contains details about the general process, as well as advice (and warnings) when it comes to both traditional publishing and self-publishing. I personally have much more experience with self-publishing, which is why the course will mostly focus on that.

(You can find my work under my real name, Tiamo Pastoor. Older work is Dutch, newer work is all English.)

Prerequisites: Something to publish.
Category: this course is part of Creative Writing.
Publishing Creative Writing

File Formats

When it comes to publishing, services generally accept or output 4 formats. Word documents (.doc or .docx) PDF documents (.pdf) EPUB documents (.epub, maybe .mobi) Microsoft Word Many writers, especially those that just start out, simply create their novels in Word. This is fine. Unless you have special requirements, this is all you need.

Conclusion

Congratulations on finishing the course about publishing! Hopefully it gave useful advice about how to sell and market a book, whether it’s to a literary agent (to get published traditionally) or to your audience (when self-publishing). It’s all about removing the Writer’s Hat and putting on the Business Hat. To see your book as a product that needs to be marketed, and to make the (sometimes harsh) decisions necessary to accomplish that.
